Hamza Hameed is a frontend developer from Karachi, Pakistan, specializing in React and Next.js. He holds a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Bahria University Karachi (February 2022 – January 2026) and works as a Front-End Developer Intern at Neural Hub (October 2026 – February 2027).

He is the founder of [Outfit Edits](https://outfitedits.com), a fashion and style blog where he publishes practical, in-depth guides — and where he learned, first-hand, how Google indexing, Pinterest traffic and display-ad monetization actually work. That experience shapes this site: deep articles, honest measurements, and no thin content.

On Cozy Tiny Space, Hamza writes about renter-friendly storage and organization: the methods, the dimensions, the weight limits, and the rental rules — so readers can add storage to a small apartment without drilling, damage, or risking their deposit.
