Tiny bathrooms punish every storage mistake: one damp towel on the floor, one bottle too many on the sink edge, and the whole room feels unusable. The problem is rarely the size of the bathroom — it is that nothing has a place.

This category gives everything a place. The wall above the toilet becomes a shelf. The inside of the cabinet door becomes a holder. The shower gets a caddy that actually stays up, because it is matched to your tile instead of fighting it.

Humidity is the constraint that makes bathroom storage different from every other room, so every guide here is honest about it: which adhesives survive steam, which suction cups hold, and when to use a freestanding unit instead of trusting a sticker.
