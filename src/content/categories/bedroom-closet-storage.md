Bedroom storage fails in the same three ways: the closet is too small, there is no closet at all, or there is nowhere to put folded clothes. This category covers all three — with fixes that come out cleanly when you move.

The trick is to stop thinking of the closet as the only storage in the room. The space under the bed, the back of the door, the wall beside the wardrobe and the top of the dresser are all storage, once you give them a job. A second hanging rod, a set of shelf dividers and one under-bed box can hold more than a second wardrobe — for a fraction of the cost and none of the damage.

Every guide gives you measurements, weight limits and the rental-friendly limits of each method, so you can check a fix against your actual bedroom before you buy anything.
