This is the method category — the techniques behind every fix on the rest of the site. The room guides tell you what to build; these guides tell you how to attach it to a wall you do not own, without damage.

That is a narrower question than it sounds, and it deserves honest answers. Adhesive strips have real weight limits. Tension rods fail on the wrong surfaces. Suction cups do not hold on textured tile, no matter what the packaging says. This category exists to say those things plainly, with the numbers, so you do not learn them from a shelf falling at 2 a.m.
