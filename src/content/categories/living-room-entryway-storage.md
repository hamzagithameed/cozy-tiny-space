The living room is where a small rental shows its size first: shoes by the door, blankets on the sofa, keys on every surface. And most small rentals have no entryway at all — the front door opens straight into the living room.

This category is about giving the room a system instead of more furniture. A drop zone by the door, one piece of furniture that does two jobs, and a rule that everything has a place before you sit down. The entryway guides work even when there is no entryway to work with, because the zone is made from furniture and habit, not walls.

Every guide is written for renters: freestanding pieces, no drilling, and nothing you cannot carry out on moving day.
