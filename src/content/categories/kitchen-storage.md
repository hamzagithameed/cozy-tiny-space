Rental kitchens are small, short on cabinets, and almost never have a pantry — and you cannot drill into any of it. This category is about working with what you have: the cabinet you already own, the wall you cannot drill, the counter you cannot extend.

The guides here start from the constraint, not the product. A shelf riser works because it uses vertical space your cabinets waste. A tension rod works because it turns dead air under the sink into a hanging rail. A rolling cart works because it fits the gap beside the fridge that nothing else fits.

Every guide gives you the same facts: how much space the fix needs, how it installs, its approximate dimensions, its weight limit, and what to do when it does not work. Read the pillar guide first for the full picture, then pick the one fix that matches your kitchen's actual problem.
